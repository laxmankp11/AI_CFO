# 📖 AI Business OS - Getting Started Handbook

Welcome to **AI CFO** — the operating system for business owners. 

Unlike traditional ERP or accounting software that forces you to navigate complex menus, sub-ledgers, and journal entries, AI CFO is built around a single promise: **You run your business by talking to your AI executive team.**

---

## Chapter 1: Welcome to Your AI Business Operating System

Traditional accounting software requires manual data entry and produces retrospective reports ("what happened last month"). 

AI CFO is a **System of Intelligence**:
1. **Conversational Accounting**: Speak or type naturally in plain English or Hindi.
2. **Deterministic Double-Entry Ledger**: The AI does not guess math. It extracts structured data and routes it to an immutable double-entry core.
3. **Predictive Projections**: The system continuously forecasts cash runway, revenue, GST liability, and operating risks.
4. **Prescriptive Action Cards**: You receive ranked recommendations with explicit `[Why?]` explainability drawers and one-tap execution.

## Chapter 2: Your First Day

Setting up your business takes less than 5 minutes:

### Step 1: Open the Mobile Executive Workspace
Launch the mobile app or browser portal. On your first login, your **AI CFO** will greet you conversationally:
> *"Hi! I'm your AI CFO. Let me take you on a 60-second tour of your workspace..."*

### Step 2: 5-Step Guided Setup
1. **Business Name**: Enter your registered business name (e.g., *ABC Electronics Pvt Ltd*).
2. **Industry**: Select your business type (Retail, Services, Manufacturing, Wholesale, CA Firm).
3. **GST & Tax**: Enable GST tracking and select filing frequencies.
4. **Bank Account**: Connect your bank or set your starting cash balance.
5. **Data Import / Demo Mode**: Import existing invoices or explore using our pre-seeded **ABC Electronics Demo**.

### Step 3: Invite Your Accountant
AI CFO is built for both business owners and accountants. Business owners use the **Mobile Executive Workspace** for daily voice interactions; accountants can switch to the **Desktop Master Data & Journal View** for compliance auditing.

## Chapter 4: Understanding Your Executive Workspace

Your workspace is organized into simple, jargon-free executive cards:

- **Business Health Score (0–100)**: A composite score combining liquidity, collection efficiency, and profitability.
- **Cash Available**: Your total liquid cash and bank balance.
- **Money Earned Yesterday**: Total revenue recognized yesterday.
- **Money Spent Yesterday**: Total cash outflows and expenses recognized yesterday.
- **Cash Runway (Days)**: How many days your cash will last at current operating burn rates.
- **Top 5 Priority Action Cards**: Ranked recommendations sorted by **Financial Impact × Urgency × Confidence**.

### The Explainability `[Why?]` Drawer
Every recommendation includes a **Why?** button. Clicking it reveals an auditable report:
- **Why?**: The exact mathematical rule or policy that triggered the recommendation.
- **Data Used**: Ledger balances, invoice history, and burn rates used in the calculation.
- **Assumptions**: Key assumptions (e.g., 5% sales growth, 92% collection efficiency).
- **Confidence Score**: Statistical confidence level (e.g., `98%`).
- **Alternatives**: Safer fallback options (e.g., *"Delay hiring by 1 month to preserve cash"*).

## Chapter 5: AI Safety, Policy Gates & Verification

Financial software earns trust through zero-hallucination reliability:

1. **Deterministic Financial Math**: The AI LLM never performs arithmetic. Projections are computed by a pure Python `FinancialModel`.
2. **Human Approval Queue (Policy Engine)**: Any transaction or action exceeding company safety thresholds (e.g., transactions > ₹1,00,000 or new vendor creation) is placed in the **Approval Queue**. No money or journal entry is posted without explicit user consent.
3. **Simulation Sandbox**: All "What-If" scenario simulations run in a isolated deep-copy context. **Nothing ever touches or alters your live accounting ledger.**
4. **Tenant Data Isolation**: Complete row-level database separation guarantees your financial data is strictly private and invisible to other businesses.

## Chapter 6: The 5-Minute Daily Routine

Make AI CFO part of your morning coffee routine:

1. **08:30 AM — Open App**: View your **Morning Brief** and Business Health Score.
2. **08:31 AM — Review Warnings**: Check red critical alerts (GST filings due, overdue receivables).
3. **08:32 AM — Execute Action Cards**: Tap `[Call]` or `[Send Reminder]` on your Top 5 Priorities.
4. **08:34 AM — Speak Transactions**: Tap the mic button and state any payments or invoices from yesterday.
5. **08:35 AM — Done!**: Close the app knowing your business is optimized and no critical task was missed.
