Frequently Asked Questions

Can the AI make mistakes?
The AI does not perform math; financial math is executed by a pure deterministic Python engine. For data extractions, any high-value transaction (e.g. over ₹1,00,000) or new vendor entry is placed in your Approval Queue for human verification before posting.
How do I correct AI if an extraction is inaccurate?
You can correct any AI extraction directly in the voice/chat interface by typing "Correct this to ₹45,000" or by clicking Reject/Edit inside your PolicyEngine Approval Queue before confirming. The AI learns from your corrections.
Is my financial data private and secure?
Yes. Every tenant database is isolated at the row/file level. Your financial records are never shared with other tenants or used to train public LLM models.
What happens during "What-If" scenario simulations?
Simulations run in a sandboxed, isolated deep-copy context. Nothing ever touches or alters your live accounting ledger. You can safely test hiring plans or sales drops without affecting your real accounts.
Can my accountant still use traditional software views?
Yes! Business owners use the 5-minute Mobile Executive Workspace, while CAs and accountants can switch to the Desktop Master Data view for full double-entry ledger audits and tax filings.
